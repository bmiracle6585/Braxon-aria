# A.R.I.A. Experience Ledger

**Document Type:** Cognitive Experience System Specification  
**Authority:** Subordinate to `Core/Persona/ARIA_CONSTITUTION.md`  
**Version:** 0.1

---

## 1. Purpose

The A.R.I.A. Experience Ledger is the authoritative record of validated experience acquired through real diagnostic, technical, operational, and future supported interactions.

The Experience Ledger allows A.R.I.A. to learn from what actually happened.

It shall preserve sufficient structured information for A.R.I.A. to determine:

- what problem occurred;
- under what conditions it occurred;
- who was involved;
- what was initially believed;
- what was observed;
- what was tested;
- which diagnostic routes were useful;
- which hypotheses were contradicted or eliminated;
- what the confirmed cause was;
- what corrective action was performed;
- whether that action produced the expected result; and
- how confidently the outcome was validated.

The Experience Ledger is not conversation history.

Conversation may provide evidence for a case.

The case record is the structured experience derived from the interaction.

---

## 2. Experience Is Stored by Case

The fundamental unit of experience shall be the **Case**.

A case represents one bounded problem, investigation, or event from initial condition through its final known state.

Example:

CASE:
EXP-00001847

USER:
Clayton

DOMAIN:
Microwave Backhaul

MANUFACTURER:
Nokia

PRODUCT FAMILY:
Wavence

PRODUCT:
UBT-T

FREQUENCY:
18 GHz

SCOPE:
Commissioning

PROBLEM:
RSL Discrepancy

CONFIGURATION:
2+0 Diversity

FINAL CAUSE:
Incorrect Radio B Configuration

RESOLUTION:
Configuration Corrected

OUTCOME:
RSL Restored

VALIDATION:
Confirmed
